FICTIONAL CURE TERMS
Equity cure: the sponsor may contribute cash that is added to EBITDA and to available cash flow for the quarter tested, up to the equity cure cap in cure_terms.csv. Cure frequency: an equity cure may be used only while cures remaining in the last four fiscal quarters is at least 1. Minimum liquidity: after any voluntary prepayment the borrower must keep cash of at least the minimum liquidity in cure_terms.csv. Prepayment: a voluntary prepayment pays the stated premium and reduces trailing twelve-month debt service by the stated amount per dollar repaid. Asset sales: net proceeds of a permitted asset sale must repay debt, and the EBITDA and cash flow of the asset sold are removed from the trailing twelve-month figures. Cure cash: a voluntary prepayment and its premium are paid from cash and reduce current assets, equity cure cash is not added to current assets, and the sale cost of a permitted asset sale is paid from its gross proceeds. These terms are synthetic and apply only to this exercise.
