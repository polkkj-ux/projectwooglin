FICTIONAL POLICY ADDENDUM (TEAM-MADE SYNTHETIC FIXTURE)
Fixed Charge Coverage Ratio is trailing twelve-month EBITDA less unfinanced capital expenditures, cash taxes and distributions, divided by trailing twelve-month fixed charges (scheduled interest and principal), minimum 1.10. Current Ratio is current assets divided by current liabilities at quarter end, minimum 1.25. A covenant the lender designates as springing is tested only for a quarter in which revolver utilization (revolver borrowings divided by revolver commitments) is at least 35% at quarter end. This addendum was written by the hackathon team to extend the synthetic exercise; it is not part of the organizer-supplied policy.
