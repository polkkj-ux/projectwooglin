FICTIONAL STATEMENT DEFINITIONS (TEAM-MADE SYNTHETIC FIXTURE)
EBITDA means operating income plus depreciation and amortization. Gross debt means the sum of the revolving loan, term loan and finance lease liability balances at period end. Debt service means interest expense plus scheduled principal payments on the term loan and finance leases. Available cash flow means EBITDA minus cash taxes paid, minus unfinanced capital expenditures, plus the decrease (or minus the increase) in operating working capital. Unfinanced capital expenditures means purchases of property and equipment paid in cash, excluding equipment acquired under finance leases. Operating working capital means accounts receivable plus inventory minus accounts payable and accrued liabilities. Trailing twelve-month amounts are the sum of the four most recent fiscal quarters, and balance sheet amounts are taken at the last quarter end. These definitions were written by the hackathon team for the synthetic statements in data/statements; they are not part of the organizer-supplied policy.
