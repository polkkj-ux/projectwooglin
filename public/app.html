Data sources

Monitoring setup

Records, then policy, then definitions and thresholds, then calculations, then investigation.

    Borrower financials

    A CSV with one row per borrower and quarter. Required columns: record_id, borrower_id, period (for example 2026-Q3), currency (USD), debt_usd, ttm_ebitda_usd, ttm_debt_service_usd, ttm_available_cashflow_usd, max_leverage, min_dscr. Optional: ttm_capex_usd, ttm_cash_taxes_usd, ttm_distributions_usd and min_fccr for fixed-charge coverage; current_assets_usd, current_liabilities_usd and min_current_ratio for the current ratio; revolver_commitment_usd and revolver_drawn_usd for springing tests. Maximum 4 MB.

    Loan policy

    A TXT file is read as-is and replaces both the sample policy and the team addendum. A PDF is sent to the LlamaParse document parser if the server has a key; on this public demo PDF parsing is switched off. You review the text before any test runs. Maximum 3 MB.

    Review the extracted policy text

    Compare this with the signed agreement. Parsers can misread tables and numbers. You can edit the text before running the tests.

    Covenant terms

    Choose how each covenant applies across the portfolio. Results recalculate on the server.

    How each covenant is tested

    Maintenance: tested every quarter. Springing: tested only when the revolver is drawn at or above the trigger. Not applied: left out of the results.

      Monitoring rules

      Internal rules for the attention list. They are settings of this tool, not covenant terms, and they never change a covenant result.

      Flag a passing test when its headroom is below this share of the covenant limit. At 5.0%, a 1.20x minimum is flagged below 1.26x, and a 3.00x maximum above 2.85x.

      % of the limit

      Flag a test that applies this quarter when its headroom fell by at least this many percentage points of the limit since the previous quarter. Example: from 8.4% to 2.6% is a fall of 5.8 points.
