Covenant Designer

Load a borrower’s financial statements and get a recommended covenant package: which tests to include, at what level, and whether each should be maintenance or springing. Every figure is calculated by the backend from the lines you supply.

1 · Financial statements

A CSV with two columns, line_item and amount_usd: trailing-twelve-month income and cash flow lines, period-end balance sheet lines. Save an Excel sheet as CSV. Parentheses or minus signs for outflows are fine.

2 · Cushion

How far the borrower’s results could fall before breaching a covenant. A bigger cushion gives the borrower more room; a smaller one gives the lender an earlier warning.

Assumptions and limits of this tool

    Data & policy sources

    Borrower data (CSV)

    Required columns: record_id, borrower_id, period (for example 2026-Q3), currency (USD), debt_usd, ttm_ebitda_usd, ttm_debt_service_usd, ttm_available_cashflow_usd, max_leverage, min_dscr. Optional: min_fccr with ttm_capex_usd, ttm_cash_taxes_usd, ttm_distributions_usd; min_current_ratio with current_assets_usd, current_liabilities_usd; revolver_commitment_usd and revolver_drawn_usd for springing tests.

    Policy document (PDF or TXT)

    TXT is read locally. A PDF is sent to LlamaParse and may use your account credits. You review the extracted text before any checks run.

    Review extracted policy text

    Parser output can contain errors. Compare it with the original agreement. You can edit it here.
